Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy just one product instead of a bundle?▾
Yes. EventFloorPlans, Signcast, and Shift can each be purchased on their own. Bundles simply combine them at a lower combined price, with one onboarding and one bill. Most venues start with a bundle because the tools work better together.
Why is onboarding charged separately?▾
Onboarding covers real work: setting up your rooms to scale, applying your branding, configuring screens and staff, and training your team. Keeping it separate keeps your recurring price honest and predictable rather than padding the monthly fee.
How long is the agreement, and is there a discount for paying annually?▾
Software bundles use a 12-month agreement. Pay annually and you take 10% off the software portion. The Low-Upfront Full Bundle uses a 24-month agreement because the website cost is rolled into the monthly price.
Do I own my website?▾
If you purchase the website outright (one-time, or the reduced price inside the Growth bundle), it's yours. On the Low-Upfront Full Bundle, ownership of the website transfers to your venue once you complete the 24-month agreement.
What about TVs, media players, and installation?▾
Hardware — television screens, media players, on-site installation, unusual integrations, and major website revisions — is quoted separately so you only pay for exactly what your venue needs. We scope it with you before anything is ordered.
What happens when I add more staff or screens?▾
Shift has tiers for up to 25 and up to 75 active staff, so you move up a tier rather than paying per person. Signcast is priced per screen per month, so you add screens as you grow. We'll right-size everything at your walkthrough.
Is this good value compared with buying tools separately?▾
The bundles are priced below the sum of the individual products, and you get one onboarding and one bill instead of several. The bigger saving is operational: your sales, planning, staffing, and signage stop living in separate apps. Note for the business owner: any direct competitor price comparisons should be verified and dated before publishing — market prices change.
